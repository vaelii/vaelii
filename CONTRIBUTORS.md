# Contributors

Vaelii is stewarded by Vaelii LLC and built with its contributors.

This file is a **community-goodwill roll, not the legal record.** Authorship is
established by your DCO sign-offs ([`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) §9.4) and the
CLA signature database (§9.5); copyright stays with each contributor. Listing yourself
here is encouraged but entirely optional, and being absent from it takes nothing away
from your credit or rights.

## Contributors

Open a pull request and add yourself — one line, alphabetical by name or handle,
**80 characters or less**:

- **At most one link, to an identity page you own** — your GitHub profile or personal
  site.
- **An optional short, factual note** — an area you worked on, or your affiliation.

Entries over the limit may be reformatted or trimmed without notice. Your credit does
not depend on this line: it is carried by the `Co-authored-by:` trailers on release
commits, your DCO sign-offs (§9.4), and the CLA signature database (§9.5).
