(comment geneticVersionOf "(geneticVersionOf ?unqualified ?genetic) means that ?genetic holds of exactly the tuples ?unqualified holds of through genetic descent: (geneticVersionOf siblingOf (GeneticKinFn siblingOf)) says two organisms stand in the second relation when they share a genetic parent, half-siblings included, and never by adoption or choice alone. Binary. The first position takes an unqualified_kinship_predicate and the second a genetic_kinship_predicate, and each unqualified predicate has one genetic version. Which relation is the genetic version of which never changes, so the correspondence is eternal.")

(set/monotonic (functional geneticVersionOf))

(comment chosenVersionOf "(chosenVersionOf ?unqualified ?chosen) means that ?chosen holds of exactly the tuples ?unqualified holds of through a family tie the persons chose rather than descended into: (chosenVersionOf siblingOf (ChosenKinFn siblingOf)) says two persons stand in the second relation when they are family to each other as siblings by choice, with no shared parent needed. Binary. The first position takes an unqualified_kinship_predicate and the second a chosen_kinship_predicate, and each unqualified predicate has one chosen version. A chosen tie can begin and end, but which relation is the chosen version of which never changes, so the correspondence is eternal.")
