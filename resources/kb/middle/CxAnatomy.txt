;; CxAnatomy — what kinds of thing have what kinds of part. Every sentence here
;; is a `partType` claim, which is the *type-level* reading of `tangiblePartOf`: (partType bird
;; wing) says birds have wings, where (tangiblePartOf LeftWing Pingu) would say one particular
;; thing is part of another. Sees the shared upper ontology via CxUniverse;
;; CxWell sees it.
;;
;; Nothing here concludes anything about an individual, and that is the point. Going
;; from "birds have wings" to "Pingu has a wing" needs a quantifier reading — every
;; bird has SOME wing, a wing nobody has named — and inventing one is how a claim about
;; kinds silently becomes a false claim about things. `typeToInstancePred` records that
;; partType and tangiblePartOf are two readings of one relation without moving a claim between
;; them; a KB that wants the individual parts states them.

(genlCx CxAnatomy CxUniverse)
(genlCx CxWell CxAnatomy)

(partType bird feather)
(partType bird leg)
(partType bird wing)

(partType insect leg)
(partType insect wing)

(partType mammal fur)
(partType mammal leg)
