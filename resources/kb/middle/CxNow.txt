;; CxNow — what is true now: a moving, indexical present. A sentence stated here holds
;; at the time the KB is asked, and need not hold timelessly or at any other time, so
;; no context above CxNow sees what it states. Sees CxUniverse, so it inherits the shared
;; ontology. It is the present of no particular world: it sees neither CxWell nor
;; CxRealWorld, and CxRealWorldNow is the real world's present. CxWell does not see it:
;; a context opts in by placing itself under CxNow.
;;
;; The present is reached through lower bounds. A clock reading states that now is no
;; earlier than a moment, which stays true for ever after; a sentence here states no
;; exact present ("it is 3:41") and no dated present-tense fact ("today is Saturday").

(genlCx CxNow CxUniverse)
