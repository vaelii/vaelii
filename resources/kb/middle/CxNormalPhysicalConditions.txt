;; CxNormalPhysicalConditions — the states of matter of stuff at ordinary room temperature
;; and pressure, in the real world. Sees CxRealWorld, so it inherits the whole shared
;; ontology and excludes every fiction. CxWell does not see it, so the everyday contexts
;; below CxWell assume no temperature and serve a story set on Mars as well as one set in
;; a kitchen. A context opts in by placing itself under this one, and a context under
;; both CxWell and this one reads the everyday theories at room temperature.
;;
;; abstract.txt places solid, liquid, gas and plasma under substance and separates them,
;; and says nothing about which substance is in which state. A substance's state depends
;; on its temperature and pressure, so the state belongs to a theory that fixes the
;; conditions. This theory fixes the ordinary ones: a room, a kitchen, a street on a mild
;; day. A theory of another temperature states its own states beside this one.

(genlCx CxNormalPhysicalConditions CxRealWorld)
;; It sees CxUniverse through CxRealWorld, and states that edge as every middle context
;; does: the middle layer loads by file name, so CxRealWorld's own edge to CxWell arrives
;; after this file, and a CxUniverse rule firing on a fact below would have no context
;; that sees both.
(genlCx CxNormalPhysicalConditions CxUniverse)
(comment CxNormalPhysicalConditions "The physical conditions of an average temperate region on Earth's surface: gravity, temperature, pressure, etc.")

;; A phase is a state a portion of stuff is in, not a kind it is: ice melts and the
;; water goes on existing.  So each room-temperature phase is a default rule rather than
;; a genl edge, and the rule-macro suggestion to make it an edge is declined.

;; ---- the solids ---------------------------------------------------------
;; Stone, wood and glass are solid at room temperature. Glass is an amorphous solid: a
;; pane does not flow.
(set/defaultRule (set/forwardRule (implies (and (stone ?x)) (solid ?x))))
(set/defaultRule (set/forwardRule (implies (and (wood ?x)) (solid ?x))))
(set/defaultRule (set/forwardRule (implies (and (glass_stuff ?x)) (solid ?x))))
(declined_rule_macro (genl stone solid))
(declined_rule_macro (genl wood solid))
(declined_rule_macro (genl glass_stuff solid))

;; ---- the liquid metal ---------------------------------------------------
(set/defaultRule (set/forwardRule (implies (and (mercury ?x)) (liquid ?x))))
(declined_rule_macro (genl mercury liquid))
(implementationNote stuff_type_by_state_of_matter "The phases this theory gives stone, wood, glass_stuff and mercury are default rules, not genl edges, since a phase is a state a portion of stuff is in, not a kind it is. The rule-macro suggestion of each edge is recorded declined.")

;; ---- metal_stuff is solid, by default -----------------------------------
;; A default rule with its exception rather than a genl edge per known metal: a metal
;; newly found, hypothesized or made up comes out solid unless the KB says otherwise.
;; Mercury is the stated exception. The rule concludes no solid membership for a portion
;; of mercury, so the portion's liquid membership meets no solid one and nothing clashes.
;; See docs/exceptions.md.
(exceptWhen (mercury ?x)
  (set/defaultRule (set/forwardRule (implies (and (metal_stuff ?x)) (solid ?x)))))
